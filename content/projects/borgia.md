#title
Overview

#details
Borgia is a web platform for managing logistics and payments across multiple university campuses. Designed and built by 4 engineering students at Arts et Métiers ParisTech, it grew from a campus project into a real production system serving over 1,000 students across 4 ENSAM campuses (Paris, Lille, Angers, Metz).

#title
What made it special

#details
This wasn't a student prototype that lived only in a graded submission — it was deployed, maintained, and actively used at scale by the student community. Managing real financial transactions and logistics for thousands of users at multiple sites required building for reliability, not just for grades. It was an early lesson in what production software actually means: ownership, edge cases, and accountability.
